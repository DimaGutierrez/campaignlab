# One click, three purchases: what should conversion rate show?

![One click, three purchases: what should conversion rate show?](https://github.com/DimaGutierrez/campaignlab/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/discussion-attribution-v2.png)

A single click can produce several purchase events. CampaignLab keeps event count separate from converted clicks, so its click conversion rate stays interpretable.

**Which denominator would you use for your campaign report, and why?** Share one example where events / clicks would mislead a decision.

Try the fictional demo locally and compare the definitions in docs/metrics.md. This is click attribution, not unique-person measurement.
