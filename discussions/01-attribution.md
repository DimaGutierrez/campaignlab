**[Open the public browser demo →](https://diegogutierrez.pages.dev/campaignlab/)** — fictional campaigns, simulated clicks and conversions, no installation or backend connection. A single click can produce several purchase events. CampaignLab keeps event count separate from converted clicks, so its click conversion rate stays interpretable.

**Which denominator would you use for your campaign report, and why?** Share one example where events / clicks would mislead a decision.

Try the public fictional demo and compare the definitions in docs/metrics.md. This is click attribution, not unique-person measurement.
