The first version has manual spend, click links and backend conversion ingestion. These next steps are proposals, not released integrations.

**Pick one problem and describe the smallest version you would actually use:**

- A: import spend from a CSV, with validation and reconciliation.
- B: distinguish lead, trial and purchase events.
- C: compare explicit attribution models.

Tell us your workflow, the pain point and one acceptance criterion. If you want to contribute, mention backend, frontend, documentation or tests.
